# Security Policy

## 📞 Reporting a Vulnerability
If you discover a security vulnerability, please do NOT open a public issue. 

Instead, please report it securely through GitHub's native Private Vulnerability Reporting system:
1. Navigate to the **Security and quality** tab of this repository.
2. In the left sidebar under "Reporting", click **Advisories**.
3. Click the **Report a vulnerability** button to open the private advisory form.

Our team will review the report privately within 48 hours and coordinate a patch and public disclosure timeline with you.
